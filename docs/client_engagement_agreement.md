### 1. Scope of Engagement
Consultant shall provide mathematical, algorithmic, and statistical consulting services as set forth in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed by both parties. Each SOW shall constitute an independent agreement incorporating the terms of this MSA.

### 2. Intellectual Property Rights
* **Client Deliverables:** Upon receipt of full payment, Client shall own all right, title, and interest in the specific game mathematical configuration (specific paytables, reel strip symbol sequences, calibrated economic constants, and custom PAR sheets) created exclusively for Client under the applicable SOW.
* **Consultant Background IP:** Consultant retains sole and exclusive ownership of all pre-existing tools, algorithms, solver scripts, simulation frameworks, general mathematical formulas, and computational libraries (including the `panini_math` engine) used to generate the Deliverables. Client is granted a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Background IP solely as incorporated into the Deliverables.

### 3. Mathematical Rigor & Simulation Standards
* **Theoretical vs. Empirical Results:** Mathematical models produced by Consultant are formulated with exact combinatorial methods. Monte Carlo simulations are provided for empirical validation and are subject to statistical variance within calculated confidence intervals ($\pm 3\sigma$).
* **Regulatory Certification:** Consultant warrants that Deliverables are engineered in accordance with recognized industry standards (e.g., GLI-11, GLI-19, BMM Testlabs). However, final certification is granted exclusively by accredited testing laboratories and competent regulatory jurisdictions. Consultant agrees to provide reasonable assistance and response to lab queries regarding the mathematical model during the certification review window.

### 4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
Both parties agree to hold all confidential information—including game concepts, unreleased IP, financial terms, and proprietary mathematical methodologies—in strict confidence for a period of no less than three (3) years from disclosure.

### 5. Fees and Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within fourteen (14) days of issuance unless otherwise stipulated in the SOW. Project-based engagements typically require a 50% mobilization deposit upon commencement and 50% upon final delivery of verified PAR sheets.
