## Practice Area 1: iGaming, Casino & Slot Mathematics

### Package 1.1: Complete Slot Math Model Design (Turnkey)
*Target Clients: Game Studios, Content Providers, Casino Operators*
* **Description:** End-to-end mathematical architecture for new slot titles across all game mechanics (Reels, Paylines, Ways to Win, Cluster Pays, Cascading/Tumble reels, Hold & Win, Multipliers, Pick-to-Win).
* **Deliverables:**
  * Complete Theoretical Math Model (Base game + Bonus features).
  * Paytable design & symbol frequency distribution.
  * Optimized Reel Strip design (balancing hit rate, perceived near-misses, and RTP).
  * Comprehensive PAR Sheet (Probability Accounting Report) in Excel & JSON.
  * Volatility Index ($VI_{90}$ and $VI_{95}$) across multiple game cycle horizons ($10^3, 10^4, 10^5, 10^6$ spins).
  * 10,000,000+ spin Monte Carlo simulation report with distribution histograms.
* **Timeline:** 2 – 4 weeks per game.

### Package 1.2: Independent Mathematical Audit & Certification Readiness
*Target Clients: Studios preparing for GLI (GLI-11 / GLI-19), BMM Testlabs, or eCOGRA certification*
* **Description:** Independent mathematical verification of an existing game model to identify vulnerabilities, runaway volatility loops, negative house-edge edge cases, or combinatorial flaws before lab submission.
* **Deliverables:**
  * Combinatorial cross-check of stated theoretical RTP.
  * Multi-billion spin stress testing for boundary conditions and jackpot frequencies.
  * RNG uniformity & statistical tests (Dieharder, NIST SP 800-22, Chi-Square goodness-of-fit).
  * Formal Mathematical Audit Report ready for attachment to certification lab dossiers.
* **Timeline:** 1 – 2 weeks.

### Package 1.3: Math Model Re-Skinning & RTP Variant Configuration
*Target Clients: Operators and studios needing multi-market compliance (e.g. 96%, 94%, 92%, 88% RTP versions)*
* **Description:** Recalibrating existing reel strips and paytables to hit varying regulatory RTP thresholds while maintaining identical game feel, volatility profile, and hit rate.
* **Deliverables:** Complete PAR sheet family across all target RTP tiers.
